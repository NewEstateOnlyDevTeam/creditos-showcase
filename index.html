The product

What Credit OS does

1

Load the report

Today it reads the IdentityIQ 3-bureau report page, cell by cell, and checks its own math against the report's totals.

2

See the mismatches

A 3D "orbit" map puts every account on each bureau's ring. Magenta spokes mark where the bureaus disagree.

3

Learn the rule

Each finding links to the federal law behind it (FCRA, FDCPA, Reg F) with a source. It's education, not legal advice.

4

Write your own letter

Draft letters per bureau or creditor. The user reviews, signs and mails them. A tracker follows each round.

Privacy by design

  • No backend: parsing, analysis and letters all run in the browser.
  • No account, no uploads. Data stays in the browser's own storage and can be deleted anytime.
  • The SSN is typed only at print time for bureau letters and is never saved.
  • No sensitive-data server means no honeypot to breach.

What Credit OS is not

  • Not a credit repair organization, law firm or financial adviser.
  • No fees to remove anything, and no promises of deletions or score changes.
  • It encourages disputing only information the user believes is inaccurate.
  • A 4-part consent screen comes before any report is read.

Why now

  • Complaint volume is surging. The CFPB says credit reporting complaints rose 182% in 2024 against the prior two-year monthly average.
  • People already buy 3-bureau reports and monitoring, but most tools stop at a score. They don't give a plan.
  • Modern browsers can run deep, private analysis on the device itself, at almost zero serving cost.
  • Finance education is moving to creators and live streams. They need a tool to point people to. (Our view.)

Compliance plan

CROA

Positioned as self-help education software. No fees for removing anything, and no outcome promises. Counsel confirms the status of any future paid tier, including contracts, disclosures and cancellation.

FCRA · FDCPA

Users dispute only what they believe is inaccurate, incomplete or unverifiable. Citations and deadline wording (30/45-day rules) get attorney verification.

Florida + privacy

Florida credit service organization rules (Ch. 817 Part III) reviewed by counsel. Data stays local by design, and the privacy policy is finalized before launch.
