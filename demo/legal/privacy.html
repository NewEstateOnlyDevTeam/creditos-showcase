Short version: your credit report is read by your browser on your device. We do not receive it, do not store it on a server, and do not sell it. Clearing your browser’s site data deletes it.

1. Who we are

CREDIT/OS is operated by [LEGAL ENTITY NAME], [MAILING ADDRESS]. Privacy contact: dvndigitalmarketinginfo@gmail.com.

2. What stays on your device

When you load a report file, the page reads it inside your browser. The parsed report, your dispute progress, your profile details (name, address and date of birth, which you may enter to fill letters), and the consent you give are saved in your browser’s local storage. This data is not sent to us or to any other server by the tool.

3. What we receive

The tool does not send your report, your profile or your letters to us. The site is served as static files. Like any web host, the hosting provider may keep standard server logs (such as IP address, time and page requested) for security and operations: [HOSTING PROVIDER AND LOG RETENTION: to confirm].

If you email us, we receive what you send. Please do not email your Social Security number, full report or account numbers.

4. Cookies and tracking

The tool does not use advertising cookies or third-party analytics [CONFIRM BEFORE LAUNCH: none are currently included]. It uses browser local storage only, so you can remove everything through your browser settings or the tool’s own delete option.

5. Sharing

We do not sell or rent your information. We do not share it with credit bureaus, creditors or collectors. The only links that leave the site are the ones you click.

6. Your choices

  • Delete your saved report and progress from the tool, or clear this site’s data in your browser.
  • Decline the consent step and you will not be able to use the tool.
  • Request access to, or deletion of, any information we hold about you, by emailing us. We will verify your identity before acting.

7. Your credit file

Your credit file is held by the consumer reporting agencies. Your rights under the Fair Credit Reporting Act are described on the Your rights page.

8. Children

CREDIT/OS is for adults aged 18 or older and is not directed to children.
