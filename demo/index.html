CREDIT/OS

Understand your credit report, and learn how to write your own letters.

Free. Private. You do it yourself, the way federal law lets you.

  1. Get your 3-bureau report from IdentityIQ and open the full report page (TransUnion, Experian and Equifax side by side). IdentityIQ is a paid third-party service; CREDIT/OS is not affiliated with it and earns nothing from it. Your reports are free from each bureau at AnnualCreditReport.com, but this tool can only read the IdentityIQ page format today.
  2. Save the page: press ⌘ S on Mac or Ctrl S on Windows, and save it as Webpage (.html).
  3. Drop the file below. CREDIT/OS checks every cell for conflicts between the bureaus and plans your disputes in rounds.

No report handy?
  • 🔒 Your report never leaves this device. It is read by your browser and saved only in this browser’s storage. You can delete it anytime.
  • 🆓 Free, no account. Credit repair companies may not charge you before their promised services are fully performed (15 U.S.C. § 1679b(b)), and you can always dispute errors yourself for free.
  • ⚖️ Not legal advice. The findings are reasons to ask the bureaus to verify, not proof of error. Only dispute what you believe is inaccurate. No result is guaranteed.
